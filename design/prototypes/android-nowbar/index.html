How it maps (Android media session): play/pause = Mic on/off (also the headset button) · previous/next = pick a station · title = the selected station · subtitle = its state, plus who hears you · artwork = station colour · two custom buttons = Mute and Solo for that station. Solo is exclusive (as in the app): it dims the others and only that station hears you. It doesn't switch the mic on; the big Mic button does. The system allows about 5 buttons, so per-station Hears you stays in the app. Layout, sizes and shapes belong to Samsung's player; we only supply the content and icons.
